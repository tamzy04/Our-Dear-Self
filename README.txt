OUR DEAR SELF — WEBSITE STARTER

Files:
- index.html: complete responsive website preview.

How to preview:
1. Download and unzip our_dear_self_website.zip.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or another browser.

Before launching for customers:
- Replace the sample products with your actual products and prices.
- Connect real checkout/payment links to product buttons.
- Connect an email marketing service if you want to collect newsletter sign-ups.
- Add your real contact email, privacy policy, terms, and refund/digital-download policy.
- Test on phone and desktop.

This is a front-end starter website. The product buttons and newsletter form are demo interactions and do not process payments or store email addresses yet.
